WHAT IS THE GAME?

Egoist Curiosity is a game made by Brazilians on Roblox. The game is inspired by the manga Blue Lock, a soccer anime where the protagonist seeks to be the best player in the world. The game tends to be based on the manga, pitting players against themselves in soccer matches with different characters. Each character has their own unique abilities and ultimates that can be used in matches. The team that scores the most goals wins, but the game also has its own unique features, including various other modes for players to enjoy.

The game has a community of over 5,000 members on Discord, with several influencers making videos about the game and posting content. The game's creator, Nico, is always posting sneak peeks of the game on the Discord server, keeping the audience informed about new additions to the game.
