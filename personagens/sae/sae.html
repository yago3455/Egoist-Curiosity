SAE

Sae Itoshi is an 18-year-old who plays for La Real in Europe. At just 18 years old, he is already considered one of the best Japanese players, being quite skilled.

Sae plays as a midfielder and has a younger brother who doesn't really like him. Sae, in Egoist Curiosity, was inspired by the manga Blue Lock created by Muneyuki Kaneshiro.

Facts

01

Sae was born on October 10, 2000, in Kamakura, located in Kanagawa Prefecture, Japan.

02

Sae prefers to play by passing the ball to strikers to score goals, but in the past he dreamed of being the best striker in the world.

03

Sae is 1.80 meters tall.

04

t the start of the series, Sae is in Japan only to renew his passport, but after hearing about Blue Lock, he decides to stay. He later joins the Japan U-20 team to face off against the Blue Lock Eleven team in the Japan U-20 match. After the Japan U-20 match concludes, Sae returns to Spain but is joining the newly constructed Japan U-20 team in the PIFA U-20 World Cup as part of Buratsuta 3, so he can challenge the Spain U-20 and finally triumph over his longtime adversary, Bunny Iglesias.

05

Sae Itoshi is a prodigious football player who is known as the best player in Japan. Sae is also a member of the New Generation World XI, as well as the Re Al youth team. He is the older brother of Rin Itoshi.

06

Sae is a tall, lean young man with reddish-brown/maroon hair and slim teal eyes that are framed by an array of long underlashes just like his brother, Rin. During the National Representative match, he wears the Japan U-20 White #10 jersey with a black shirt underneath the jersey and some black gloves.

07

Since a young age, Sae has been cold, blunt, and serious. He has only ever cared about becoming the best Midfielder in the world and has only had time for things that get him closer to his goal. Sae can also be arrogant and condescending, looking down on others even if they are older than him and wield organizational authority. He is egotistical in his football play but has shown to be able to restrain himself and play his position, not trying to outshine others unnecessarily.

08

Sae is prideful as a football player, looking down on Japanese football and all who participate in it. Sae states that he would much rather die or play in Europe with a bunch of college students than play in the J-League or play on the Japan National Team. He is very confident in his skills and wholeheartedly believes that nobody in Japan is worthy of his skills as a teammate. He dislikes the fact that he was born in Japan, saying things like he was simply born in the wrong country.

09

Though Sae is a difficult person, he is not impossible to work with. He, out of pure interest, decides to stay in Japan on a whim after hearing about the Blue Lock Project and even joins the Japan U-20 for the match against the Blue Lock Eleven. But when his interest is satisfied, he has no problem abandoning those he deems unworthy of his time.

CURIOSITY

01

He is part of the prestigious New Generation World XI, which brings together the world's most promising under-20 players.

02

Before going to Europe, Sae was originally a lethal striker. He changed his position to midfielder after realizing he needed to control the game more broadly.

03

He is able to use Metavision, an ability that allows him to have a panoramic field of vision and predict the movements of his opponents with perfection.

04

His playing style focuses on analyzing the opponent's physical and tactical weaknesses, disrupting the enemy's play through irreplaceable and precise passes.

05

He is Rin Itoshi's older brother. Their relationship is extremely tense and competitive, as Rin harbors a lot of resentment towards Sae for giving up on their shared dream of becoming the best strikers in the world.

06

Although he is arrogant and often criticizes the level of Japanese football, he recognizes Yoichi Isagi's talent and shows a certain respect for his development.

07

Real Inspiration: Sae's look and playing style are believed to have been inspired by former Japanese professional soccer player Hiroshi Nanami, who played as a midfielder.

08

His favorite food is Ichibo steak (Japanese sirloin), he loves bodybuilding, but hates cream-based desserts and dry, sliced ​​french fries.

09

The name "Sae" (冴) literally means "to be clear," "to be serene," or "brilliant," which perfectly reflects his surgical composure on the field and his ability to analyze the game without emotion. The surname "Itoshi" (糸師) can be translated as "master of strings," a brilliant metaphor used by the author to show how he acts as a true maestro, controlling all the players and passes in the game as if they were puppets.
